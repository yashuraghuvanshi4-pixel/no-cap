System Capabilities

From fragmented records to actionable intelligence

Everything an investigation desk needs to move from raw data dump to a ranked suspect network.

Multi-source ingestion

Normalizes FIRs, CDR call graphs, ledger transactions, surveillance logs and open-source intel into one unified knowledge graph.

Entity extraction

Named-entity recognition identifies persons, locations, vehicles, phones, shell organizations, events and monetary flows from free text.

Relationship mapping

Multi-relational graph edges encode communications, co-location, financial transfers, supervision and attendance across the network.

Influencer scoring

Degree, betweenness and eigenvector centrality combine into an influence metric that surfaces the coordinating kingpins of a syndicate.

Anomaly detection

Rule and ML heuristics flag circular fund flows, high-risk buried nodes, CDR pivots and temporal escalation patterns.

Actionable intelligence

Path tracing, entity inspectors and a live alert feed turn raw data into investigative leads ready for field action.

Analysis Pipeline

Six stages, zero manual graph building

An automated, auditable pipeline that mirrors how an intelligence analyst reasons — just much faster.

01

Collect

Ingest multi-source intelligence — FIRs, CDRs, financial records, surveillance, social media, criminal history and agency reports.

02

Extract

NLP entity extraction pulls people, locations, vehicles, phone numbers, organizations, events and financial instruments from unstructured text.

03

Map

Graph analytics build a relationship map showing how every entity connects — communication links, financial flows, movement and co-location.

04

Score

Centrality algorithms (degree, betweenness, eigenvector) rank influencers and compile composite risk scores for triage.

05

Detect

Machine-learning anomaly detection flags unusual patterns — circular transfers, escalation timing, hidden hubs and cross-border traces.

06

Assist

Visual and analytical insights deliver actionable intelligence directly to investigators for further action.
